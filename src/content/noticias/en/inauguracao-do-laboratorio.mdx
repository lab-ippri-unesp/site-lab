The **IPPRI/UNESP Multi-user Laboratory** now has a public website to present its
computing infrastructure for the **Digital Humanities**, its team and the terms of shared
use.

On the website you can explore the [available equipment](/en/equipamentos/) —
high-performance servers (CPU and GPU), scalable storage and data-analysis platforms — and
learn [how to request access](/en/acesso/), on-site or remote.
