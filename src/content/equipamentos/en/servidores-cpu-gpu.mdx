Redundant, **high-performance** CPU and GPU servers for processing, indexing and analyzing
large volumes of Digital Humanities data. GPU capacity enables machine-learning tasks and
the training of open-source language models.

The infrastructure runs on a **Proxmox virtualization cluster**, in continuous operation
and expansion at IPPRI/UNESP.
