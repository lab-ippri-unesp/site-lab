A platform for **information aggregation and research** in a continuous, real-time flow,
based on **Open Semantic Search** and **OpenSearch**. It offers indexing, search, analysis
and visualization of large databases, with **public query** services over indexed
databases — in line with the laboratory's Open Science principles.
