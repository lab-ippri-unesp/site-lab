## What it is

The **IPPRI Multi-user Laboratory** is a shared research infrastructure of the
Institute for Public Policy and International Relations at UNESP. It brings together,
in a single space, equipment, methods and technical support for research, teaching and
outreach projects of the institution and its partners.
