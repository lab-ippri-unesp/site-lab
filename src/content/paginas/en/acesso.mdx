Access to the equipment can be **on-site or remote**. Opening hours are **9:00 am to
5:00 pm**.

## Who can use it

Users fall into two categories:

**1. Faculty and students of partner institutions**
They have access to the laboratory's equipment, services and spaces. To register:

- Send proof of formal affiliation to **unesplabri@gmail.com** and request the
  registration form.

**2. People without a formal link to the partner institutions**
They must:

- Obtain the consent of one of the **Steering Committee** professors or of users;
- Send the consent form to **unesplabri@gmail.com** and request the registration form.

## Training and rules

The laboratory offers **regular training** on how the equipment works and on the usage
rules, delivered by the technical team. The usage rules are clearly and accessibly
available on this website.

## Costs and counterpart

There is currently **no operating-cost sharing** — UNESP covers all costs. Instead, a
**collaboration counterpart** is requested: making the data generated available in the
laboratory's databases, whenever there is no legal impediment.

## Acknowledgement in publications

Publications resulting from projects supported by the multi-user equipment must
**acknowledge IPPRI/UNESP** and the funding agencies and other public or private bodies
that enable the acquisition and full operation of the equipment used.
